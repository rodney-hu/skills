# Templates and Worked Example

Names and details are made up. Replace every placeholder with real research.

**Copy format:** every letter lowercase except the letter I. That includes sentence starts,
names, company names, acronyms, subject lines, and sign-offs. "lmk" is allowed for "let me know".
This applies to the copy blocks only. The notes around them are normal case.

**Be specific:** name the actual buyer or audience in the copy.

- Not this: "25 companies that fit your best buyer"
- This: "25 mid-size logistics companies hiring dispatchers"

**Offer lines (pick one):** "happy to walk you through how I built it" / "lmk if you want more info" / "lmk if it's relevant"

## Loom script (about 2 min, one take)

| Beat | Length | What to do |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 1. Noticed | ~25 sec | Show their page. Say what you saw. Say the hypothesis with care. |
| 2. Winners | ~30 sec | One pattern from similar companies. |
| 3. Built | ~45 sec | Run the asset. Show the result before explaining the tool. |
| 4. Offer | ~20 sec | Say what is simulated. Invite a correction. One easy next step. Stop. |

Say their name and the specific thing in the first 5 seconds. Show their page first, then
the asset. Loom title: "[name], quick look at [thing]".

## Real reference: Rodney's own LinkedIn DM

Sent as three separate messages. Copy this rhythm, not the exact words. This one is shown
as originally sent, with its original capitalization. New copy follows the lowercase
format above.

```
Message 1 (6:42 AM)
I put together a quick prototype for an onboarding/education piece for you guys: [link]

Message 2 (6:44 AM)
Could warm up leads who aren't ready for a demo, or help new schools keep students logging in after week two

Message 3 (7:06 AM)
I can walk you through it if you want
```

What makes it work:

- Message 1 is the asset and the link. No greeting, no biography.
- Message 2 is the so-what: two concrete uses in their world, phrased as "could."
- Message 3 is the offer, alone, and soft.
- Short and plain, like a text. No sign-off, no emojis, no hype.
- Messages 1 and 2 went out two minutes apart. Message 3 came about 20 minutes later.

## DM template (LinkedIn or X): 3 messages

Use for any DM. Send each as its own message.

```
Message 1
put together a list of [number] [specific buyer or audience, in plain words] for [you / your team]: [link]

Message 2
could [concrete use 1], or [concrete use 2]

Message 3
[pick one: happy to walk you through how I built it / lmk if you want more info / lmk if it's relevant]
```

If the asset needs context, put the signal in message 2: "saw [specific thing], so I [what you made]. could [use]."

## Cover message: DM (Loom)

Send as 2 messages. The ask lives in the video, so there is no separate offer message.

```
Message 1
made you a quick video about [specific thing]: [link]

Message 2
[one line on what's in it, or the asset you built for them]
```

## Cover message: email

rodney

## Follow-up (4-7 business days, one only)

```
hey [name], one more thing from the video: [one useful screenshot or takeaway]. here's the link again: [link]. no worries if the timing's off.
```

## Written email (no Loom)

Use when the asset speaks for itself and the channel is email. One message.

```
subject: [asset name] for [company]

hi [name],

I was looking at [specific thing] and noticed [what I saw].

I checked a few [similar companies]. the main pattern is [one insight].

I made [asset] for you based on that: [link]

if it helps, I can walk you through it. lmk.

rodney
```

## Worked example

**Company:** a 10-person B2B SaaS. **Offer:** lead list and email workflow.

- **Signal:** careers page shows an SDR role open for 3 months (source URL, date).
- **Observation:** the SDR role has been listed for 3 months. The site speaks to three different buyers.
- **Hypothesis:** outbound may still be founder-led. Unconfirmed.
- **Winners pattern:** similar teams that book meetings pick one buyer and send to one tight list.
- **Asset:** one-page list of 25 companies that fit one buyer, each with a contact and a reason to reach out (public data).
- **Owner:** the founder.

**Loom, beat 1:** "hey sam, it's rodney. I was looking at your careers page and saw the sdr
role has been open a while. I also noticed your site speaks to three different buyers:
logistics, retail, and healthcare. so I'm guessing outbound is still mostly on you.
I could be wrong about that."

**Cover DM (Loom), 2 messages:**

```
Message 1
made you a quick video about your outbound: [link]

Message 2
there's a list of 25 mid-size logistics companies hiring dispatchers in it
```
