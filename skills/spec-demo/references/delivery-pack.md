# Delivery pack: link, Loom script, note

Same pack for every recipe. Format and rhythm come from `job-to-demo/references/templates.md`.

## `loom-script.md` (about 2 minutes, one take)

| Beat | Length | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Noticed | ~25 sec | Name the company and the role. Show their site: who they help. |
| 2. Understood | ~30 sec | The role's KPI and the audience's pain, framed as a guess ("my guess is..."). Quote one line from the brief. |
| 3. Built | ~45 sec | Open the live link. Show the asset working before explaining anything. |
| 4. Offer | ~20 sec | Say what is mocked (no emails sent, no payments, public data only). Invite a correction. One easy next step. Stop. |

Add a "Before you hit record" list: what to have open, which rows or sections to hover, and any claim not to say out loud (from the brief's claims to avoid).

## `note.md` (the message Rodney may send)
All lowercase except the letter I. Plain, short, like a text. Two or three separate messages:

```
message 1
made a quick [asset in plain words] for [their audience]: [live url]

message 2
could [use 1 tied to the role's KPI], or [use 2]

message 3
[pick one: happy to walk you through how I built it / lmk if you want more info / lmk if it's relevant]
```

Rules: name the real audience, product or accounts. "could" and "might", never claims of results. No unmeasured numbers. One ask. No meeting request in the first touch.
