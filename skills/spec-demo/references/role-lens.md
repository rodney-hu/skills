# Role lens: research the person, not just the company

Input is a title, never a named person. No contact data, no enrichment tools.

## Searches (2 to 3, save to `research/search/role-<topic>.json`)
1. `"<title>" <industry> challenges OR priorities` (LinkedIn posts, interviews, conference talks)
2. `"<title>" job description <industry>` (what they are hired and measured for; job posts list KPIs plainly)
3. Optional: `site:reddit.com "<title>" OR "<plain-language version of the title>" <pain>`

## Fill the brief's Role lens section
- **Measured on:** 2 to 3 KPIs, from job posts first.
- **Blamed for:** what lands on their desk when it slips.
- **What they say:** 2 to 4 quotes with sources.
- **What they could forward:** the one asset they'd send up the chain.
- **Router hint:** role family and default recipe from `router.md`.

## Use it in the copy
- The asset name and hero speak to the prospect's audience, not to the role. The role lens decides *which* asset and *which* outcome leads; the page itself still talks to their audience.
- Exception: `buyer-signals` speaks to the role directly, because the role is the audience.
- Put the role's KPI in the Loom script and the note, in plain words.
