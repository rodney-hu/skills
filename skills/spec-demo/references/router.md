# Router: pick one recipe for this role

The role decides what "valuable" means. A marketing lead values leads. An education lead values activation. A founder values who to call next. Pick the asset the role could forward to their boss.

## Step 1: default by role family

| Role family | Typical titles | Measured on | Default recipe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing / growth / demand gen | CMO, VP or Head of Marketing, Growth, Content, Demand Gen | leads, pipeline, CAC | `lead-magnet-page` (or `interactive-tool` if their buyers compare numbers) |
| Education / onboarding / CS | Head of Education, Clinical Education, Onboarding, Customer Success, Training | activation, time to value, retention | `onboarding-eec` |
| Sales / BD / founder | Founder, CEO, VP Sales, Head of BD, Partnerships | pipeline, who to call | `buyer-signals` |
| Revenue / ecommerce / offer owner | Head of Revenue, Ecommerce, Product Marketing for a paid offer, course creators | conversion, AOV | `checkout-page` |
| Unclear or several | | | Score all five below |

## Step 2: score (1 to 3 each, multiply)

| Factor | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Role KPI fit** | The asset moves the role's main KPI directly | Helps someone else's KPI |
| **Company gap** | The site has no such asset (no free entry point, no onboarding content, no public tool) | They already have a strong one |
| **Proof available** | The brief has audience quotes and their own claims to build from | Thin research; would need invention |
| **Build cost** | Layout exists in the kit (status "live") | Layout must be built first |

Highest product wins. **Tie-break:** strongest proof in the brief, then the one Rodney can explain fastest on camera.

## Recipe one-liners (for the card)

| Recipe | The link shows | Strong when |
|---|---|---|
| `lead-magnet-page` | A Biggest Mistakes opt-in page for their audience | They sell to a defined audience with no free entry point |
| `onboarding-eec` | A page that teaches 5 short lessons their new users need | Signups or students stall after week one; education or CS roles |
| `buyer-signals` | A table of up to 10 named accounts likely in market now, each with sourced signals | B2B with a clear buyer title; founder or sales roles |
| `interactive-tool` | A working calculator, self-assessment or scorecard, with an embed snippet | Their buyers justify a purchase with numbers or a readiness check |
| `checkout-page` | An offer page for one audience type, ending in a mock checkout | They sell a paid offer and the current page talks to everyone at once |

## Hard rules
- One recipe per card. Write the runner-up on the card with the reason it lost.
- Never pick a recipe whose done checks need data you can't get from public pages.
- If no recipe scores above 8, say so on the card and recommend parking the prospect.
