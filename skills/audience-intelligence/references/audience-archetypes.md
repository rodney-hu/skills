# Audience Archetypes — Asset B (Viewer / Buyer Side)

*The identity and stage the audience occupies. This is who you're writing TO. Match 3+ signals before assigning.*

---

## The Ambitious Striver
**Identity:** "I know I want more. I just don't know the path."
**Stage:** Early-to-mid career or early entrepreneurship. High motivation, low traction.
**Signals in channel content:** "How to get started with…", "If you're just beginning…", motivational + tactical mix, broad how-to topics
**Core Tension:** Wants the destination, doesn't trust the journey
**What activates them:** Seeing someone "like them" succeed; clear next step

---

## The Frustrated Expert
**Identity:** "I'm good at what I do. So why am I invisible?"
**Stage:** Skilled operator or specialist who lacks distribution, brand, or visibility
**Signals in channel content:** "If you're great at what you do but…", positioning content, personal brand, distribution-as-leverage
**Core Tension:** Skill gap is solved; visibility gap is killing them
**What activates them:** Seeing peers with less skill but more visibility get the wins

---

## The Reluctant Beginner
**Identity:** "I don't think I belong here. But I'm trying anyway."
**Stage:** New entrant intimidated by the space. Imposter syndrome dominates.
**Signals in channel content:** "Beginner-friendly", "you don't need [credential]", encouragement-heavy, low-jargon
**Core Tension:** Wants permission to start before they feel ready
**What activates them:** Permission-giving content; "you don't need to be an expert to start"

---

## The Disillusioned Pro
**Identity:** "I've tried this before. I'm skeptical it works."
**Stage:** Has attempted similar solutions, didn't work, scarred by gurus
**Signals in channel content:** Anti-guru framing, "the truth about…", debunking, receipts-driven proof
**Core Tension:** Wants to believe again but won't be fooled twice
**What activates them:** Specific evidence, contrarian-but-credible takes, no-BS tone

---

## The Aspiring Operator
**Identity:** "I'm a thinker. I want to become a builder."
**Stage:** Sees the gap between strategy and execution; wants to ship things
**Signals in channel content:** "Stop planning, start building", systems content, MVP framing, anti-overthinking
**Core Tension:** Knowledge has outpaced action — paralysis from option overload
**What activates them:** Permission to ship something messy; constraint-based frameworks

---

## The Overwhelmed Generalist
**Identity:** "I know too much. I can't focus."
**Stage:** Consumed too much content, can't pick a direction, suffers from optionality
**Signals in channel content:** "Stop consuming, start doing", focus content, single-thread framing, anti-shiny-object
**Core Tension:** The information they wanted has become the thing keeping them stuck
**What activates them:** Forced-choice frameworks, permission to ignore everything else

---

## The Burned-Out Achiever
**Identity:** "I got the results. They cost too much."
**Stage:** Has succeeded by traditional metrics but the lifestyle isn't sustainable
**Signals in channel content:** "Success without burnout", lifestyle design, leverage > hustle, time-as-currency framing
**Core Tension:** Wants to keep the wins but change the cost structure
**What activates them:** Frameworks that promise compounding without grinding

---

## How to Assign

1. Match 3+ signals to a single archetype
2. If the channel serves multiple, name the primary × secondary (e.g. "Frustrated Expert × Aspiring Operator")
3. Always include one sentence of reasoning in Asset B

> Example: *"Audience Archetype: The Frustrated Expert. Channel consistently addresses skilled operators who lack visibility — content rarely covers craft (assumed mastered) and instead focuses on positioning, distribution, and personal brand."*

---

## Connection Notes

The audience archetype determines:
- **What pain to lead with** (surface pain — the one they'll admit)
- **What fear to name carefully** (deep fear — the one that builds loyalty when named accurately)
- **What identity to honor** (current state) and what identity to promise (aspirational)
- **What proof format works** (Disillusioned Pros need receipts; Reluctant Beginners need permission)

Get this right and every piece of copy lands harder.
