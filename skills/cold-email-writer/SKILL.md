# Cold Email Writer

Writes subject line + body for any cold outbound email following the Pre-Sold Protocol SOP.
Every output routes to the right angle, applies universal rules, and self-checks before delivery.

---

## Step 1 — Identify the Angle

Pick the right angle before writing. If it's not clear from context, ask.

| Angle | When |
|---|---|
| **Pre-Sold Protocol** | Outreach to a specific prospect with a conversion gap. Has a Trigger field. |
| **Offer Validation** | Testing a new offer or positioning. No hard sell. Feedback-first frame. |
| **Follow-up** | Touch 2–4 in a sequence. Same thread. Different angle — not a re-pitch. |

→ Read the matching file in `references/angles/` before writing.

---

## Step 2 — Gather Inputs

Minimum required:
- **Prospect first name**
- **Trigger** — the specific conversion gap or reason for outreach
- **Audience** — who they serve (pull from Audience Intelligence if in CRM)

For Pre-Sold Protocol: pull Trigger + Audience Intelligence + Company Intelligence from the CRM record.
For Offer Validation: pull from what the user describes about the market or offer being tested.
For Follow-up: pull the prior email and any engagement signal.

If any of these are missing, ask before writing.

---

## Step 3 — Apply Universal Rules

These are non-negotiable on every email. Full rules in `references/cold-email-sop.md` Module 3.

**Length:** 25–50 words body. Hard cap 100 words total.
**Format:** One sentence per paragraph. Line break after every sentence. No dense blocks.
**Reading level:** 4th grade. Short words. Short sentences. Active voice.
**What's banned:** Em dashes, bold text, bullets, links in first touch, exclamation points.
**No openers:** Never start with "I hope this finds you well", "Sorry for the cold email", or any pleasantry.
**No AI tells:** Never use "moreover", "furthermore", "leverage", "streamline", "optimize", "synergize", "crucial", or similar.
**One ask only:** Low-friction, interest-based question. Never a meeting request in first touch.
**Sign-off:** First name only. No title, no company name, no signature block in first touch.
**Subject line:** 1–5 words. Lowercase or title case. Reads like an internal message, not a pitch.

---

## Step 4 — Write and Output

### Choosing a Variation (Pre-Sold Protocol)

Read `references/angles/pre-sold-protocol.md` and pick the variation that fits. There are six:

| Signal | Variation |
|---|---|
| Clear conversion gap, asset ready | 1: Core Formula |
| Something genuinely impressive to acknowledge | 2: Icebreaker + Compliment |
| Small batch, selective positioning | 3: Scarcity / Selection |
| Validating a segment, no pitch yet | 4: Market Research |
| Asset already built, lead with the deliverable | 5: Pre-Built Asset |
| Audience is the strongest entry point | 6: Audience-First |

Default to Variation 1 or 6 when unsure.

Every variation uses the same structural logic: pull from Trigger + Audience Intelligence + Company Intelligence and write fresh — never copy the examples verbatim. All placeholders must be replaced with real research before outputting.

For offer validation and follow-up angles, see the other reference files.

### Self-Check Before Outputting
- Body under 100 words?
- Every sentence its own paragraph?
- No em dashes, no bold, no links?
- No banned words (moreover, leverage, streamline, etc.)?
- Capital I used throughout?
- One ask only, not a meeting request?
- All placeholders replaced with real research?
- Signed with first name only?

Fix anything that fails before delivering.
