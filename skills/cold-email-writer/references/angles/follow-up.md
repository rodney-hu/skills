# Angle: Follow-Up Sequence (Touches 2–4)

## When to Use
When a prospect hasn't replied to Touch 1. Send in the same thread. The goal is not to re-pitch, it's to give them a different reason to respond.

Each touch should shift the angle meaningfully. If Touch 1 called out the friction, Touch 2 might show a result. Touch 3 might share a relevant resource. Touch 4 closes the loop cleanly.

---

## Touch 2: Social Proof or Consequence (Day 3–5)

**Frame:** A different angle on why this matters. Could be a result from a similar client, or a reframe of the consequence from Touch 1.

**Structure:**
- One sentence: a specific result or a consequence reframed
- One sentence: soft re-offer ("Still happy to send it over if relevant.")

**Rules:**
- Under 30 words
- Don't re-state the problem from Touch 1 word-for-word
- If citing a result, make it specific and real

**Example:**
> A mindset coach in a similar niche sent this to her list and had 40 people reply in the first 48 hours.
>
> Still happy to send it over if relevant.

---

## Touch 3: Resource Share (Day 8–10)

**Frame:** Share something genuinely useful. A neutral third-party resource, a relevant insight, or a market observation. Not a Trojan horse, actually useful.

**Structure:**
- One sentence: frame the resource or insight
- One sentence: why it's relevant to their situation
- CTA: "Worth a look?" or "Thought this was relevant."

**Rules:**
- Under 35 words
- The resource should be genuinely good, don't fabricate or make up a source
- This is the lowest-friction touch, no pitch at all

**Example:**
> There's a breakdown of how coaches in the identity/transition space are structuring their opt-in offers that's worth 5 minutes.
>
> Happy to send the link if you want it.

---

## Touch 4: Breakup (Day 14–18)

**Frame:** Acknowledge you haven't heard back. Assume timing is off, not that they're uninterested. Leave the door open cleanly. This touch often gets the most replies because it's honest.

**Structure:**
- One sentence: close the loop, assume timing not lack of interest
- One sentence: leave the door open (reply-to-reopen mechanism)

**Rules:**
- Under 30 words
- No guilt, no pressure
- Never say "I'll stop bothering you", too self-deprecating
- Make it easy to re-engage: "just reply here" or "just let me know"

**Example:**
> Figured I'd close the loop, probably just bad timing on my end.
>
> If priorities shift, just reply here and I'll send it over.

---

## Sequence Spacing Reference

| Touch | Days After Prior Touch | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Touch 1 | Day 1 | Core friction + offer |
| Touch 2 | Day 3–5 | Social proof or consequence reframe |
| Touch 3 | Day 8–10 | Resource share or market insight |
| Touch 4 | Day 14–18 | Breakup / timing-off close |

---

## Rules for All Follow-Up Touches
- Always reply in the same thread, don't start a new email
- Each touch must shift the angle, not repeat the pitch
- Never ask for a call in follow-up touches
- Keep follow-ups shorter than Touch 1, not longer
- If a prospect opens multiple times or clicks a link, flag them for manual review, don't auto-follow-up as normal
