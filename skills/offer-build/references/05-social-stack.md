# Reference: Twitter / LinkedIn Ghostwriting Offer Stack
**Load when:** Building any offer for Twitter ghostwriting, LinkedIn ghostwriting, social content creation, or personal brand content services.

---

## CONTENT BUCKET FRAMEWORK

Every social ghostwriting offer is built around three content buckets:

| Bucket | Purpose | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Reach | Appeals to widest audience | Life/career advice, broadly relatable |
| 2 — Domain Expertise | Niche authority building | Tactical, actionable, industry-specific |
| 3 — Thought Leadership | Frontier positioning | Industry insights, trends, forward-looking takes |

Each bucket gets its own CTA aligned to a relevant product, program, or opt-in.

## DELIVERABLES LIST — Full Offer Stack

### Onboarding and Strategy
- Profile audit: headshot, header, bio rewrite for high-quality reader attraction
- Positioning audit: review past content, name and claim category, incorporate into bio
- Content bucket identification: 30-min onboarding call (interview style), assign 3 buckets
- Biographical pillar post (pinned post): claim niche, give lay of the land, drive to opt-ins

### Content Creation
- 9 short-form content templates (3 per content bucket) — proven containers
- 3 long-form content templates: actionable (how-to), analytical (data/frameworks), aspirational (story)
- Weekly short-form content: 40 pieces/month (Mon–Fri, 2 slots/day)
- Weekly long-form content: 8 pieces/month (Tues + Thurs)
- Cross-platform repurposing: every Twitter thread → LinkedIn carousel
- CTA templates: 3 templates aligned to client's opt-ins/products/services

### Posting and Scheduling
- Consistent publishing calendar: 8:30AM short-form (M–F), 11AM long-form (T/Th), 7PM short-form (M–F)
- Cross-post management across platforms

### Engagement and Community
- Positive engagement: weekly audit, extract top 5 comments, feed into next week's content strategy
- Negative engagement: monitor, respond to confrontational comments, hide/remove abusive content
- Community growth hacking: 50 relevant creators identified, ~1hr/day commenting on their recent content

### Analytics and Optimization
- Weekly and monthly analytics review: pull top 3 performers per content bucket, produce takeaways memo
- Evergreen high-performing library: archive of proven topics, hooks, threads — ready to resurface or hand off

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC NOTES

**Twitter:**
- Short-form = individual tweets
- Long-form = threads
- Tool: Hypefury can auto-append CTAs when posts hit engagement thresholds

**LinkedIn:**
- Carousel format performs strongly — repurpose Twitter threads as visual carousels
- Long-form = LinkedIn articles or extended text posts
- Algorithm note: external links perform better in comments than in post body

**Cross-posting rule:**
Content cannot be copy-pasted between platforms. Twitter thread formatting ≠ LinkedIn formatting.
Carousels require a design step (Canva template). Always reformat before cross-posting.
