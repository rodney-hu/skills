# Reference: Newsletter Ghostwriting Offer Stack
**Load when:** Building any offer for newsletter ghostwriting, newsletter management, or newsletter growth services.

---

## CLIENT ARCHETYPES — Target Archetypes 1 and 2 First

| Archetype | Profile | Primary Pitch Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Has traffic, no newsletter | Active on social, no email list | "You're building on rented land. Every week without a newsletter is subscribers never captured." |
| 2 — Has newsletter, underperforming | Publishing but bad results | "You already have the asset. We just need to make it work." |
| 3 — Not monetizing (secondary) | Big audience, not converting | Upsell on existing clients — FOMO campaigns, product launch emails |
| 4 — Free newsletter, could go paid (emerging) | Strong free list | Future upsell — paid tier strategy |

**Archetype 2 failure signals to look for:**
- Giant unbroken paragraphs, no subheads (impossible to skim)
- Abstract ideas with no steps or takeaways (not actionable)
- Last issue published 3+ months ago (no cadence)
- Named after self or broad topic (no audience clarity)
- Text only, no visual support

---

## PROBLEM INVENTORY — Newsletter Side

```
NL-01  No clear positioning (generic name, no defined reader, no value exchange)
NL-02  No content buckets (opens blank page weekly, no system)
NL-03  No consistent publishing cadence
NL-04  Wrong or no newsletter platform (ConvertKit / Substack / Beehive selection unclear)
NL-05  No high-converting opt-in page (buried on website, no dedicated page, no offer)
NL-06  No newsletter template (every issue formatted differently, no repeatable structure)
NL-07  No subject line strategy (defaults to clever vs. clear, no A/B testing)
NL-08  No visual strategy (text-only, misses visual learners)
NL-09  No inbox engagement management (reader replies unanswered)
NL-10  No analytics review process (data not used to inform strategy)
NL-11  No reader surveys (zero structured feedback mechanism — almost no one does this)
NL-12  No evergreen content library (no archive of proven topics and subject lines)
```

## PROBLEM INVENTORY — Social Side (Tier 2 Only)

```
SC-01  Social bio not optimized for newsletter conversion
SC-02  No pinned post driving newsletter sign-ups
SC-03  Newsletter not repurposed into social content (every issue = 10–20 tweets, 4–8 threads)
SC-04  Newsletter not cross-posted (Medium, blog, Quora — easy triple-dip)
SC-05  No social CTAs driving newsletter sign-ups
SC-06  No community growth hacking (commenting on relevant creators to drive traffic)
```

## DELIVERABLES LIST

### Tier 1 — Newsletter Autopilot Package

**Setup and Strategy**
- Clarify newsletter positioning (target reader, value exchange, name if needed)
- Define 3–5 content buckets
- Establish publishing cadence
- Set up or optimize newsletter platform

**Content and Publishing**
- Write, edit, publish one newsletter/week (4/month)
- Build branded newsletter template (3 variations, client selects)
- A/B test two subject lines per issue
- Source and place imagery (stock or custom graphics)

**Conversion Infrastructure**
- Build dedicated high-converting opt-in page
- Write offer copy with reader benefit bullets and issue previews

**Engagement and Analytics**
- Monitor inbox: respond to positive replies, handle negative replies professionally
- Monthly analytics review + recommendations memo
- Quarterly reader survey (design, send, analyze)
- Build and maintain evergreen content library (top issues, best subject lines, top topics)

### Tier 2 — Newsletter Acceleration Package
Everything in Tier 1, PLUS:

**Social Amplification**
- Profile audit: optimize bio and links for newsletter conversion
- Write and publish pinned post driving sign-ups (Twitter + LinkedIn)
- Extract 10 short-form posts/week from newsletter; schedule and post
- Append 3 posts/week with newsletter CTA (manual or Hypefury automation)
- Extract 2 long-form threads or LinkedIn posts/week; post with CTA
- Cross-post each newsletter to: Medium, website/blog, Quora
- Create 3 standard reusable CTAs for social rotation
- Community growth hacking: engage 50 relevant creator comment sections weekly

**Pricing rule for this vertical:**
Tier 1 and Tier 2 contain DIFFERENT work (not just more volume).
Do NOT apply a per-unit price break between tiers.
Price Tier 2 at a true 2x premium.
