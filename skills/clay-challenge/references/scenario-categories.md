# Build-a-GTM-System Brief: Template and Categories

## The template (fill this in for every brief)

```markdown
# [Company Name]: [Role/Objective] Challenge

**Scenario:** You've just [joined/been hired at/been brought in to help] [Company],
[one sentence establishing what they do + one concrete signal — stage, funding,
a recent event, a product detail]. [If real company: note which facts are real
vs. constructed for the exercise.]

**Objective:** [One sentence, one deliverable — e.g. "Identify the top 10
[accounts/candidates/companies] and build [outreach/a scored list/a brief] for
each."]

**Time box:** [X] minutes. Build only in Clay — one attempt, no pre-built
templates.

**Deliverables:**
1. **Define the ICP** — not just "a good [target]," the *ideal* one.
2. **Validation signals** — [3–5 signals appropriate to the category, see below].
3. **Scored/ranked list** — top N built from those signals.
4. **Personalized [message/brief]** per record — must reference at least one
   enriched signal, not generic filler.

[If practice-only against a real company, add:]
> *This is a practice exercise. Any message drafted here is for skill-building
> only — not to be sent.*
```

## The four categories

Rotate through these rather than repeating the same one back-to-back. Pick based on what fits a named company, or choose deliberately for variety.

### 1. Lead-gen & prospecting
**Frame:** "You've been hired as a GTM engineer at [Company]. Build a prospecting system that identifies and reaches [target accounts/buyers]."
**Typical validation signals:** firmographics (headcount, industry, funding stage), technographics (tools they use, integrations), intent signals (hiring for a relevant role, recent funding, product launches), fit signals (existing customer lookalikes).
**Deliverable 4 becomes:** a personalized cold outreach message per account/contact.

### 2. Hiring & recruiting
**Frame:** "You've been hired as a GTM engineer at [Company]. Help them hire a [role] by building an end-to-end sourcing and screening workflow." (This is the actual 2025 Clay Cup finals format — EchoStage hiring a Head of Sales.)
**Typical validation signals:** current/past titles, tenure per role (not a job-hopper, but not stagnant either), number and caliber of companies worked at, direct competitor/industry experience, location/time-zone fit, visible activity (LinkedIn posts, conference talks).
**Deliverable 4 becomes:** a personalized outreach/InMail message per candidate.

### 3. Market research & competitive intel
**Frame:** "You've been hired as a GTM engineer at [Company]. Leadership needs a scored view of [the competitive landscape / expansion targets / a market segment] before [a decision point — board meeting, pricing change, market entry]."
**Typical validation signals:** funding/growth trajectory, headcount changes, pricing/positioning moves, recent product launches, review-site sentiment, hiring patterns that signal strategy.
**Deliverable 4 becomes:** either a short synthesized insight brief (if the audience is internal) or a personalized message if the exercise involves reaching out to companies (e.g. partnership or acquisition scouting).

### 4. Event ops & partnerships
**Frame:** "You've been hired as a GTM engineer at [Company], which is planning [an event/partnership push]. Build a system to identify and reach the right [sponsors/speakers/attendees/partners]."
**Typical validation signals:** audience overlap, past event participation, budget/sponsorship history, relevant audience size, topical fit.
**Deliverable 4 becomes:** a personalized invite/pitch message per prospect.

## Picking a category for a real company

If Rodney names a real company without specifying a category, look for the strongest signal in what you find during research:
- Visibly hiring for a GTM/sales/leadership role → **Hiring**
- No visible outbound/lead-gen motion, small team → **Lead-gen**
- Recently funded, competitive space, or a "we need to understand the market" vibe → **Market research**
- Runs or sponsors events, has a community/partner program → **Event ops**

State the pick and the one-line reason in the brief's scenario line — don't silently decide.

## Writing the model solution (on request only — see SKILL.md Step 6)

When Rodney asks to check his work, write the reference in the same 4-part shape as the brief, but as one strong worked example rather than instructions:

```markdown
## Model solution: [Scenario name]

**Example ICP:** [1-2 sentences — specific, not generic]

**Example validation signals** (with why each matters here):
- [signal] — [one line on why it's high-signal for this scenario]
- [signal] — [one line]
- [signal] — [one line]

**Scoring logic:** [how you'd weight/combine the signals into a ranking]

**Example message:** [one full message showing real personalization —
references a specific enriched detail, not a mail-merge field]

*This is one strong approach — Clay Cup itself scores reasoning quality,
not a single right answer. Check whether your logic holds up, not whether
you matched this exactly.*
```

Keep the example message realistic for the category — a candidate InMail reads differently than a partnership pitch or an account-based cold email. Match the register to what a real practitioner in that category would actually send.
