# Brand Archetypes — Asset A (Creator Side)

*How the creator positions themselves in the market. Match 3+ signals before assigning.*

---

## The Sage / Expert
**Market Position:** "I know more about this than you, and I'll teach it to you clearly."
**Signals:** Tutorials, frameworks, deep dives, "how it actually works", credentials cited, measured tone
**Tone:** Authoritative, precise, methodical
**Examples:** Ali Abdaal, MKBHD, Thomas Frank

---

## The Challenger
**Market Position:** "Everyone else is doing this wrong. Let me show you why."
**Signals:** Contrarian titles, expose-style content, pushback against industry norms, bold claims
**Tone:** Direct, provocative, confident, unapologetic
**Examples:** Dan Koe, Codie Sanchez, Alex Hormozi

---

## The Guide / Mentor
**Market Position:** "I've been where you are. Let me walk you through it."
**Signals:** Step-by-step content, empathetic framing, "here's what I wish I knew", story-driven
**Tone:** Warm, patient, encouraging, relatable
**Examples:** Pat Flynn, Vanessa Lau, Matt D'Avella

---

## The Builder / Operator
**Market Position:** "I build the things I teach about. Here's the receipt."
**Signals:** Build-in-public content, real metrics shared, process documentation, no-fluff reputation
**Tone:** Pragmatic, results-oriented, no-BS
**Examples:** Greg Isenberg, Pieter Levels, Starter Story

---

## The Storyteller
**Market Position:** "Story is the most powerful vehicle for what I want to teach."
**Signals:** Narrative-driven videos, personal journey arcs, high emotional engagement, vulnerability
**Tone:** Conversational, intimate, compelling
**Examples:** Yes Theory, Casey Neistat, Nas Daily

---

## The Connector / Community Builder
**Market Position:** "We're figuring this out together. You're not alone."
**Signals:** Audience-first content, Q&A format, "you asked so I answered", responds to community in content
**Tone:** Inclusive, warm, conversational
**Examples:** Lavendaire, Muchelleb

---

## The Transformer / Coach
**Market Position:** "You are capable of more. I'm here to call it out of you."
**Signals:** Mindset content, before/after framing, accountability language, high-stakes calls to action
**Tone:** Motivational, direct, urgent
**Examples:** Ed Mylett, Lisa Bilyeu, Tom Bilyeu

---

## How to Assign

1. Match 3+ signals to a single archetype
2. If split: name primary × secondary (e.g. "Builder × Challenger")
3. Always include one sentence of reasoning in Asset A

> Example: *"Brand Archetype: The Builder × The Challenger. Documents real builds with real metrics (Builder) while consistently pushing against guru-style advice in their niche (Challenger)."*
