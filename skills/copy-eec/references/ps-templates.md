# PS (Postscript) Monetization Templates

## Strategic Deployment

**Days 1-2:** Soft sell (awareness, social proof)
**Days 3-4:** Medium sell (value proposition, bonuses)
**Day 5:** Strong sell (urgency, final call)

## The 11 PS Templates

### PS #1: There Will Never Be A Perfect Time

**When to use:** Day 1
**Purpose:** Address procrastination

**Template:**
```
P.S. — There will never be a perfect time to {take action}. 

The best time to start was 6 months ago. The second best time is right now.

If you want to {get result} without {making these mistakes}, {paid offer} can help.

{One-sentence description of what it does}.

{Link or CTA}
```

**Example:**
```
P.S. — There will never be a perfect time to start fundraising.

The best time to start was 6 months ago. The second best time is right now.

If you want to raise your first round without scaring away investors, The Fundraising Accelerator can help.

It's a 12-week program that walks you through finding angels, crafting your story, and closing your first checks.

Learn more here: [link]
```

### PS #2: Customer Success Stories

**When to use:** Day 2
**Purpose:** Social proof

**Template:**
```
P.S. — {Customer name} was {struggling with X}.

After {using your product/taking your course}, they {achieved specific result}.

Here's what they said: "{Brief testimonial quote}"

If you want similar results, check out {paid offer}: {link}
```

### PS #3: Refund Policy/Guarantee

**When to use:** Day 3
**Purpose:** Remove risk

**Template:**
```
P.S. — {Paid offer} comes with a {X-day} money-back guarantee.

If you don't {get specific result}, just email me and I'll refund you. No questions asked.

That's how confident I am this will work for you.

{Link or CTA}
```

### PS #4: Resource Sneak Peek (Part 1)

**When to use:** Day 3
**Purpose:** Preview value

**Template:**
```
P.S. — Inside {paid offer}, you'll get {specific resource}.

This alone is worth {value/price/time saved}.

{One sentence explaining what it does}.

{Link or CTA}
```

### PS #5: Resource Sneak Peek (Part 2)

**When to use:** Day 4
**Purpose:** Stack value

**Template:**
```
P.S. — Yesterday I mentioned {resource 1}. But that's just one piece.

You also get:
- {Resource 2}
- {Resource 3}
- {Resource 4}

{Link or CTA}
```

### PS #6: Bonuses Reminder

**When to use:** Day 4
**Purpose:** Increase perceived value

**Template:**
```
P.S. — If you join {paid offer} before {deadline}, you also get:

- {Bonus 1}: {What it does}
- {Bonus 2}: {What it does}
- {Bonus 3}: {What it does}

Total value: ${amount}. Yours free when you join by {deadline}.

{Link or CTA}
```

### PS #7: FAQ

**When to use:** Day 3 or 4
**Purpose:** Address objections

**Template:**
```
P.S. — Quick FAQ about {paid offer}:

Q: {Common question}
A: {Clear answer}

Q: {Common question}
A: {Clear answer}

Still have questions? {Link or CTA}
```

### PS #8: Testimonial

**When to use:** Day 4
**Purpose:** Social proof + specificity

**Template:**
```
P.S. — "{Longer testimonial quote that includes specific results and transformation}"

— {Name, Title/Company}

Want results like this? {Link or CTA}
```

### PS #9: Limited Spots/Urgency

**When to use:** Day 5
**Purpose:** Create urgency

**Template:**
```
P.S. — Quick heads up: {Paid offer} only has {X} spots left.

Once they're gone, the next cohort doesn't start until {date}.

If you want to {get result} before {timeframe}, grab your spot now: {link}
```

### PS #10: Deadline Reminder

**When to use:** Day 5 (morning/midday)
**Purpose:** Final urgency

**Template:**
```
P.S. — The {bonus/discount/limited spots} for {paid offer} expires tonight at midnight.

After that:
- {What they lose #1}
- {What they lose #2}
- {What they lose #3}

Don't miss out: {link}
```

### PS #11: Final Call To Action

**When to use:** Day 5 (final email)
**Purpose:** Last chance

**Template:**
```
P.S. — This is the last email in the series—and your last chance to {benefit}.

{Paid offer} closes in {X hours}.

If you're ready to {transformation}, here's the link: {link}

If not, no worries. I hope the free course helped.

Either way, thanks for reading.
```

## Customization Guidelines

**Match PS to email topic:**
If Day 3 covers "Going straight to VCs," your PS could mention "The Accelerator shows you how to find 50 angel investors in your city."

**Keep it natural:**
Don't force every PS. If the flow feels awkward, skip it for that day.

**Progressive disclosure:**
- Day 1: Mention it exists
- Day 2-3: What's inside
- Day 4: Social proof + bonuses
- Day 5: Urgency + final call

**One PS per email:**
Never stack multiple PS elements. Pick the most relevant one.

## Example Deployment Schedule

**Day 1:** PS #1 (Perfect Time) → Soft intro to paid offer
**Day 2:** PS #2 (Success Story) → Social proof
**Day 3:** PS #3 (Guarantee) OR PS #7 (FAQ) → Remove friction
**Day 4:** PS #8 (Testimonial) + PS #6 (Bonuses) → Stack value
**Day 5:** PS #11 (Final CTA) → Close

## When NOT To Use PS

- List building only (no paid offer)
- Pure authority building
- User explicitly requests no monetization
- Testing content before adding offers
